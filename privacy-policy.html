Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 4, 2026

Dream Arena - Football Ground Booking App ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

To provide football ground booking and team management services, we may collect:

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address
  • Account authentication information
  • Player profile information and statistics
  • Team membership information
  • Football ground booking details
  • Reviews, ratings, and comments
  • Profile pictures and uploaded ground images
  • Device and technical information required for app operation

User Accounts

Users must create an account to access application features. Account information is used for authentication, managing bookings, joining teams, maintaining player profiles, and providing personalized services.

Football Ground Booking Information

The application stores booking-related information such as selected grounds, booking dates, time slots, and booking status to provide reservation services.

Teams and Leagues

Users may join teams, participate in leagues, and manage football-related profiles. Team information, player details, and league participation data may be stored to provide these features.

Reviews and Ratings

Users can submit reviews, ratings, and comments about football grounds. This information may be displayed to other users to improve the booking experience.

Camera and Photo Library

Dream Arena may request access to the camera and photo library to allow users and authorized administrators to:

  • Upload profile pictures
  • Upload football ground images

Photos are accessed only when users choose to upload them.

Location Information

Dream Arena does not collect precise GPS location information. Users can browse and select football grounds available through the application.

Payments

Dream Arena does not process payments and does not collect financial or payment information.

How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Provide football ground booking services
  • Manage teams and leagues
  • Display reviews and ratings
  • Improve application performance
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse

Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide application functionality or comply with legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

Dream Arena is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
