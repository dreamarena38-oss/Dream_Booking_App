Delete Your Dream Arena Account

If you want to delete your Dream Arena account and associated data, you can request deletion by contacting us.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

Email: Dreamarena38@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Dream Arena account and request account deletion.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account information
  • Name and profile information
  • Player profile data
  • Team membership information
  • Booking history associated with the account
  • Reviews and ratings created by the user

Data Retention

Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, or administrative purposes. Any retained data will be deleted when it is no longer required.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe after verification of account ownership.
